## Reproducible annual volume scenario

- TAM = 171,486,900 person-trips × **assumed** 25% eligible ÷ **assumed** four people = 10,717,931 group-trip opportunities.
- SAM = TAM × **assumed** 1% venue reach = 107,179 group-trip opportunities.
- SOM = **target** 250 verified services/month × 12 = 3,000 services/year.
- Eligibility of 10–40% gives TAM 4.29–17.15 million. These are scenarios, not customers, market share or traction. The mixed-city questionnaire does not estimate Shanghai eligibility.

## Proposed USP test

Compare the prototype with chat plus maps on identical inputs. Record hard-limit violations, time to all-member agreement and private member ratings. Zero violations, better minimum-member ratings and 20% less agreement time are future pilot targets, not measured outcomes.

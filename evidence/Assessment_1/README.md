## Survey scope at a glance

The supplied export has **156 records and 34 questions**. After the recorded self-screening, **132 main responses** remain. The final deck uses a **38-response descriptive subset** with 3–5-hour answers and explicit same-group duplicates removed. Nineteen of those 38 are unsure whether another group member has responded; these are not 38 verified independent groups or a representative Shanghai sample.

The subset reproduces **20/38 budget constraints (52.6%)**, **30 minutes median coordination time from 31 numeric answers**, and **18 definitely / 18 possibly willing to try**. [The method and cell crosswalk](Survey_Statistics/Survey_Method_and_Findings.md) preserve the final deck's other reported statements separately from directly reproduced measures. Concept interest is not a completed product trial or a payment commitment.

## Publication scope

The full public workbook retains all 34 question answers and 156 record rows. IPs, exact submission timestamps, completion duration and recruitment-source metadata are excluded; the introduction's contact email is masked. The supplied original is unchanged. Five declined-consent rows contain administrative screening status only and are excluded from research analysis.

The current business scenario is **display advertising plus sponsored listings**, following final slide 5. Prices, impressions, sales quantities and staffing are explicit assumptions. AI/cloud methods on slide 4 are proposed integrations; the published offline prototype is the separate inspectable baseline. The records distinguish calculations, reported work, implemented rules, proposals and measured outcomes.

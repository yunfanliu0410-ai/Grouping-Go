## What the source supports

The official product article describes AI search and comparison, recommendations, and execution of local-service tasks such as orders, taxi requests and bookings after user confirmation. It supports the capability summary in the slide.

## Our application or calculation

Grouping-go proposes a shared itinerary in which each member enters limits and confirms a plan. The intended comparison is whether that workflow helps groups check a complete outing against all members’ limits.

## Scope and current project status

A product introduction cannot establish that Meituan lacks every form of trip planning or group coordination. No controlled comparison with Meituan has been completed. The slide’s gap is a project research question, not a verified statement that the competitor cannot provide the function.

**Status:** Vendor-described capabilities verified; the proposed advantage remains to be tested.

**Reading note:** Avoid expanding the gap into the absolute claim that Meituan only supports individual shops or ordering.

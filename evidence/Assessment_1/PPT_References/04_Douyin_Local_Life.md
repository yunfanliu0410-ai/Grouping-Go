## Our application or calculation

The intended competitor in this Shanghai-focused project is Douyin (抖音). Grouping-go proposes agreeing on the timing of a multi-stop outing rather than only discovering or purchasing an individual offer.

## Scope and current project status

Douyin and the international TikTok product must not be treated as interchangeable. This source verifies Douyin’s mainland-China local-life services; it does not verify the same feature set for TikTok. It also does not prove that all group timing or route features are absent from Douyin.

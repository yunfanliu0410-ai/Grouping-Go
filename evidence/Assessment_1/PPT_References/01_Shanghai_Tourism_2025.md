## What the source supports

The official table reports 17148.69万人次 of visitors from other Chinese provinces and municipalities in 2025, equivalent to 171,486,900 person-visits, an increase of 11.9%. The slide rounds this to 171.5 million.

## Our application or calculation

The half-day share (25%) and four people per group are project assumptions. Using the unrounded official figure: 171,486,900 × 25% ÷ 4 = 10,717,931.25, or approximately 10.72 million group-outing equivalents per year. This is an illustrative scenario, not an observed number of eligible groups.

## Scope and current project status

The unit is visits, not unique people. The figure does not establish the number of 3–6-person groups, half-day shopping-district outings, users the project can reach, or willingness to pay. The assumed share should be tested before using this as a serviceable market estimate.

**Status:** Published market context verified; the derived group-outing estimate is an unvalidated project scenario.
